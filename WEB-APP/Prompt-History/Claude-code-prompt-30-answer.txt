## Summary

- **Total investments at 31-Dec-2025 were $63,957m**, up $4,747m (+8.0%) from $59,210m (Balance Sheet, p.125). Schedule I gives the same total (Note 21, p.213).
- **Fixed maturities, AFS dominate at $46,041m (72.0%)**. Next come mortgage loans at $6,837m (10.7%), limited partnerships and other alternative investments at $5,804m (9.1%) and short-term investments at $4,353m (6.8%). The remaining lines total $922m (1.4%).
- **Within AFS bonds, corporates are the largest block at $23,076m (half of AFS).** Structured securities follow at $16,485m (ABS, CLOs, CMBS, RMBS), then municipals at $4,652m and U.S. Treasuries at $1,381m (Note 5, p.150).
- **The bond book's net unrealized loss narrowed** from $(1,955)m to $(814)m because interest rates fell (Note 5, p.150–151). This drove the $898m AFS gain in OCI (Comprehensive Income, p.124).
- **About 93% of the fair-valued investments are Level 1/2.** Level 3 assets rose to $4,028m from $2,992m (Note 4, p.141–142, 146).

## Red flags & areas for follow-up

1. **Schedule I uses different classes from Note 5.**
   - Schedule I shows "U.S. govt & agencies" of 5,929 against U.S. Treasuries of 1,381 in Note 5, and "MBS/ABS" of 11,937 against structured securities of 16,485.
   - Both gaps are exactly **4,548**, which suggests agency RMBS is reported under government in Schedule I. This is not stated; confirm with management.
2. **Mortgage loans sit $230m above their Level 3 fair value.** This economic loss does not appear in book value or OCI.
3. **Level 3 grew 35%** (2,992 → 4,028), mainly in private corporates ($2,866m) and short-term investments (98 → 382). The unobservable-input table covers only $2,765m of the corporates, because broker-priced items are excluded.
4. **Fair value is not disclosed** for limited partnerships ($5.8bn) in Schedule I. There are also $2.6bn of unfunded commitments.
5. **The FVO book halved** and lost $(30)m in fair value (Note 4, p.147).
6. **Source and tagging oddities:**
   - The cash-flow equity-proceeds line is labelled "Marketable Securities", and "Securties" is a typo.
   - The equity statement's "Total – Treasury stock acquired (1.6)" appears to be a scaling or tagging error (the real figure is $(1,616)m).
   - In Note 4's 2024 table, foreign-exchange derivative liabilities are shown as a positive 18. The total of (12) only works if 18 nets against (30).
   - The contents of "Other investments" ($262m) are not fully broken down. Are derivative assets of $20m included?
7. **Accrued investment income** has a $10m difference that is not explained (see Balance Sheet above).
