Download a filing

Give the stock's ticker and a link to its filing on SEC EDGAR: the filing's index page (e.g. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/874766/000087476626000012/0000874766-26-000012-index.htm), or the iXBRL viewer link of its main document. The app saves the inline XBRL document (marked iXBRL under Document Format Files) and the Data Files (schema, calculation, definition, label and presentation linkbases, extracted instance) in {{ docs_dir }}\<TICKER>. The folder then appears in the report list, and the Ingest step reads it from its XBRL tags.
